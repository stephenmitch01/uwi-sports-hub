Platform Governance

Legal, Data, and Usage Guidelines

This page outlines the rules, responsibilities, and protections associated with the use of UWI Sports Hub.

Internal Use Data Governance Access Rules

Internal Use Only

UWI Sports Hub is an internal platform intended for authorized university sports operations only. It is not a public-facing athlete directory or open-access system.

Access to the platform is controlled and restricted to users who have been approved and issued accounts through the proper institutional process.

Account Access and Responsibility

User accounts are issued by platform administrators and tied to authorized institutional roles. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials and for all actions performed under their signed-in account.

Unauthorized access attempts, account sharing, or misuse of privileged access are not permitted.

Data Ownership and Institutional Control

Athlete, coach, team, and competition records managed in UWI Sports Hub remain subject to university and institutional governance. The platform serves as an operational system for authorized sports administration, recordkeeping, and reporting.

Data access is role-based and campus-scoped. Users should only access and manage records within the scope of their approved permissions.

Data Accuracy and Record Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring that data entered into the platform is accurate, authorized, and appropriate for university sports operations. This includes athlete identity data, competition records, staff assignments, and performance statistics.

Privacy and Confidentiality

Information stored within UWI Sports Hub is intended for legitimate internal use. Users must handle platform data responsibly and should not disclose, distribute, or expose records outside authorized university contexts without proper approval.

Headshots and Media Handling

Athlete and coach headshots, along with any related media used in the system, must be uploaded and managed responsibly. Media should only be used for legitimate operational and administrative purposes within the platform context.

Minor Safeguarding

Where minor athletes are represented in UWI Sports Hub, their information must be handled with appropriate sensitivity, restricted access, and role-appropriate use in line with safeguarding expectations.

Reports and Data Export

Reports generated through the platform are intended for internal university use. Users are responsible for ensuring that exported materials are handled securely and shared only with authorized stakeholders.

Acceptable Use

UWI Sports Hub must be used strictly for legitimate university sports administration and related operational workflows. Any misuse, unauthorized sharing, or attempt to bypass platform controls is prohibited.
