How Stats Flow Through the Platform

Stats are entered from the Competitions page. Search for and select the appropriate competition, then open the sport-specific score sheet, scorecard, or result sheet to add the data. Once saved, the record is connected to the competition, team, athletes, and sport.

1. Open Competitions

Go to the Competitions page, search for the correct competition, and select it before entering any stats.

2. Add the score sheet

Use the competition's score sheet, scorecard, or result sheet to enter the team, opponent, event details, athlete entries, scores, marks, or times.

3. Review connected views

After saving, the result can appear in recent results, competition pages, team pages, athlete stat pages, leaderboards, and reports.
