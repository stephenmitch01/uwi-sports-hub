UWI SPORTS HUB

UWI Sports Hub is an internal sports management platform for authorized university users. Existing users can sign in on this page. New accounts are created through private invitation links issued by platform administration.

Records and rosters Maintain athlete profiles, teams, staff assignments, and eligibility context in one connected workspace.
Results and stats Enter score sheets and sport-specific performance data that flow into athlete, team, and competition records.
Reports and decisions Review summaries that help campus sports operations track participation, performance, and data completeness.

Sign In

Use your existing account credentials to enter the platform.

Having trouble signing in? Contact your campus administrator.
Account Creation

Accounts are not created publicly. If you have received a private invitation, use the link provided to access the account creation page.

What The Platform Is For:

Built for structured internal sports administration across UWI campuses.

Athlete Records

Maintain internal athlete profiles, roster status, sport participation, and performance history.

Competition Management

Organize competitions by sport, divisions, rounds, events, matches, and result structures.

Comprehensive Stats

Support detailed sport-specific stats, retrospective score-entry workflows, and performance summaries.

Staff & Teams

Support multiple staff roles, linked teams, rosters, and structured visibility across pages.
