About the Platform

UWI Sports Hub is built to centralize internal sports records, athlete information, team structures, and competition data across university sports operations.

The platform gives authorized university sports personnel a structured place to manage athletes, coaches, teams, competitions, and sport-specific performance records in one connected internal system.

What the platform supports

Athlete Management

Internal athlete records, team assignments, status tracking, sport participation, headshots, body information, and long-term performance history.

Coach and Team Structure

Coach profiles, staff assignments, team rosters, squad organization, and connected navigation between athletes, coaches, and teams.

Competition Workflows

Competitions organized by sport, division, round, heat, event, or match with linked participants, results, and stat-entry workflows.

Detailed Statistics

Sport-specific stat systems that support N/A values, calculated metrics, retrospective score-entry, personal bests, and season or all-time views.

How stats are added and shared

Stats are added through sport-specific score sheets and result sheets, usually from a competition, team, or sport results workflow. A saved sheet stores the match or event details, the UWI team, opponent or event field, athlete entries, scores, marks, times, and calculated summaries where the sport supports them.

Because each saved result is linked to its sport, competition, team, and athlete records, the platform can reuse the same entry across connected pages. That means a cricket scorecard, basketball score sheet, or track and field result can feed recent results, team views, athlete stat profiles, competition pages, leaderboards, and reports without staff re-entering the same information in each place.

The most important rule is that people and teams should be linked correctly before or during stat entry. When an athlete belongs to more than one sport or team, those assignments help the platform place their results in the appropriate sport pages, team rosters, athlete history, and performance summaries.

Why UWI Sports Hub exists

Sports operations become stronger when athlete information, team assignments, competition outcomes, and performance records are managed in one structured internal system instead of being scattered across spreadsheets, messages, paper records, and disconnected files. UWI Sports Hub is intended to reduce fragmentation, improve continuity, support stronger administration, and make sports data easier to manage over time.

How the platform helps day to day operations

The platform is designed to support real sports department workflows, not just static record storage. It helps staff maintain organized athlete and coach profiles, manage rosters, track competition structures, record results, review sport-specific performance data, and generate a clearer picture of campus sports activity over time.

Designed for connected internal workflows

UWI Sports Hub is intended to function as one connected internal environment. Athlete pages, coach pages, team pages, competition pages, and reporting views are meant to work together so that records, assignments, and performance data can be followed across the system instead of managed in isolation.

Built for structured university sports administration

The platform is positioned as a serious internal university product for organized sports operations. It is designed to support continuity, consistency, and more reliable recordkeeping across campuses, while giving approved staff a practical environment for managing sports data with greater clarity and efficiency.
